**Phonetics**, the study of [speech](https://www.britannica.com/topic/speech-language) sounds and their physiological production and [acoustic](https://www.britannica.com/dictionary/acoustic)qualities. 

It deals with:
- the configurations of the vocal tract used to produce speech sounds **(articulatory phonetics)**.
- the acoustic properties of speech sounds **(acoustic phonetics)**.
- and the manner of combining sounds so as to make [syllables](https://www.britannica.com/topic/syllable), words, and sentences **(linguistic phonetics)**.

In Phonetics, we use the [[IPA (International Phonetic Alphabet)]], a standardized alphabetic system for describing the sounds of speech.
