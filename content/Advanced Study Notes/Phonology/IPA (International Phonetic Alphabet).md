Is an alphabetic system of phonetic notation based on primary Latin Script. 
Used as the international standard for laying out or describing in text how a word should sound.

When using the IPA, it is very important to abandon traditional spelling rules. This is essentially another language used to represent what we already speak, and the symbols have in and of themselves, structures which will help demystify the unfamiliar ways that these are used and written.
