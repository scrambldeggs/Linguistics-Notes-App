Allophones are distinct phonetic variations of a single speech sound ([[Phonemes]]). They are pronounced differently depending on their surrounding sounds or word position, but replacing one allophone with another never changes a word's meaning.

*Whether a consonant is an allophone or phoneme depends entirely on what language you are speaking. Some consonants may also serve as both. 

*Therefore, it is important to think about which word you are speaking before concluding if a consonant is an allophone or a phoneme, or both.
