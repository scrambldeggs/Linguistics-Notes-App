**Running**
- Flexion (Pump) of elbows 
- Flexion of knees 
- Shoulder extension and flexion

Skipping
- Plantar-Flexion of foot 

Hopping 
- Extension and flexion of knees
- Constant flexion of elbows 
- Plantar-flexion of jumping foot

Jumping 
- Plantar-flexion
- Shoulder extension for stability 

Lunges 
- Dorsiflexion 

Side-stepping 
- Adduction of hip/pelvic joints 
- Plantar-flexion 

Leaping 
- Pushing off with a plantar flexion and knee extension 
- Elbows are flexed to provide stability
