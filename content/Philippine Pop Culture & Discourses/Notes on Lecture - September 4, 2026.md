The difference between **Pop Culture, Mass Culture, and Folk Culture**

Folk culture is made by the people, but does not rely on mass media to regurgitate, replicate, spread, and expand it. 

Popular culture, however, does. 

Mass culture vs Pop culture is more blurred. 
Pop culture has to do with capitalism, commoditization, profitability, etc. Pop culture is a commercial movement, sold and advertised and goaled to **be** as popular as possible. 

Mass culture is not reliant on capitalism and mass media.

While the people create the popularity required for pop culture to flourish (pinapauso), the companies which seek to profit from this culture jump on the bandwagon and further precipitate enculturation.  

Pop culture is transgressive. It crosses limitations.
According to the postmodern perspective, anything can be classified as pop culture.
