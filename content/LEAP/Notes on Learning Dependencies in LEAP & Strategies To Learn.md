The 5 Core Learning Dependencies in LEAP are: 
- Reading
- Writing
- Listening
- Speaking
- Viewing

Here are the different strategies associated with each: 

Reading Comprehension
>Predicting & Clarifying 
>Questioning
>Summarizing 

Listening & Viewing 
> Analyzing Conversations
> Note-taking and Outlining
> Recognizing Facts & Opinions

Speaking in Different Situations
> Expressing Ideas & Opinions
> Applying Speaking Strategies

Writing
> Business Letters and Emails
> Paragraph Writing
> Essay Writing
