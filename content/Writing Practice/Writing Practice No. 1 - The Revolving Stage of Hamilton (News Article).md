Hamilton is a hit musical written by Lin Manuel Miranda which became popular for its unique musical profile, striking visuals, and ambitious retelling of America's early history. 

After the show's premiere sold out The Public Theatre in February of 2015, Hamilton transitioned into Broadway, eventually getting an official, filmed stage version a year later in the Richard Rogers Theatre in Manhattan, New York. 

This marked a significant, although not often discussed, addition to the set of Hamilton: the revolving, turntable stage. According to Lin Manuel Miranda during an interview with The Circle in 2016, he was "shocked yet excited" to find out that the Richard Rogers Theatre had a built in, dual revolving "donut" design to be used for the show. 

"I immediately called up Andy (Andy Blankenbeuhler, lead choreographer of Hamilton) to revise a lot of the choreo he had come up with, because we just had to use this new technology, I mean- it was amazing!" Miranda added. 

Live audiences were pleasantly surprised by the new choreography, gaining the show more traction online before the filmed studio version released on Disney+ months later. To this day, in July 2026, Hamilton is still being performed on Broadway all over the world, and it never stopped using the revolving stage.
